## Revenge of Señor Cactus 🌵

This game was developed for Xamk Game Jam Spring 2024
The game is a small 3D survival game created during Xamk Game Jam Spring 2024.

In this game you are stranded on a small island with only a toy boat. 
To survive, you must collect flowers from the sea and bring them back to your island to upgrade your shelter. 
But be careful — Señor Cactus is also sailing around and trying to steal your flowers with his cannon.

### Gameplay:

-Explore the surrounding sea with your boat

-Collect flowers and bring them back to your island

-Upgrade your shelter to survive

-Avoid Señor Cactus, who will try to shoot you and steal your flowers

-If Señor Cactus hits you with his cannon, you lose one flower

### Controls:
W A S D    Move

Mouse      Look around
